Transit Reality

Ride times & the frequency problem

Round 1 starts wherever your group begins — this report assumes Rapid Central Station — and every hiding run must end at a zone within the 60-minute hiding period. Later rounds begin from the previous hider's spot, so re-check these times from that stop rather than from the hub. One-seat rides fit comfortably; verified transfer itineraries do not (the fact-checked Kentwood transfer to Walmart-Grandville takes 92 minutes, not the 62 originally claimed).
