# The Rapid (Grand Rapids, MI) — Summer 2026 Service Guide (Compact)

Plain-text conversion of The Rapid's Summer 2026 schedule book, generated from
the agency's official GTFS feed (connect.ridetherapid.org/InfoPoint/GTFS-Zip.ashx).

- **Service period:** July 1 - August 30, 2026
- **Agency:** The Rapid (Interurban Transit Partnership), ridetherapid.org, 616-776-1100
- **Timezone:** America/New_York. **All times below are 24-hour clock** (e.g. 13:05 = 1:05 pm).
  Times past midnight wrap (e.g. a 0:15 at the end of a run is 12:15 am the next morning).
- Timetables show **timepoints only** (the lettered stops from the printed book);
  buses serve many local stops between timepoints. `-` means that trip skips the timepoint.
- Schedules shown are the majority patterns: Weekday, Saturday, Sunday. A handful of
  dates run slight variants (1-2 extra trips on some routes); Sunday July 5 and
  Saturday July 18-Aug 29 have extra DASH (Route 51) and Route 2 service on some dates.
- **No fixed-route service on Independence Day (July 4)** — only DASH Route 51 runs
  (special holiday service).

This is the compact edition: service spans, headways, and route endpoints only.
Full timetables are in THERAPID.md.

## Fares & Payment

- **Adult cash fare: $1.75** per ride. Exact change only (no change given). No transfers with cash.
- **Senior/Disabled reduced cash fare: $0.85** (proof required: Rapid-issued ID, Medicare card, or proof of age 65+).
- **Children under 3'6" ride free** with a fare-paying adult.
- **Wave card** (reloadable, $3 purchase fee, wave.ridetherapid.org):
  - **Transfers free for 1 hour 45 minutes** after first tap.
  - **Daily fare cap: $5.25** — rides after the cap are free that day.
  - **Monthly fare cap: $60** (calendar month, Wave card only).
- **Contactless payment** (Visa/Mastercard/Discover, Apple/Samsung/Google Pay) accepted on all
  fixed routes and BRT; gets the standard transfer and the $5.25 daily cap, but no monthly cap.
- College riders: GVSU students/staff ride **free** with ID; GRCC students/employees free with pass;
  Aquinas $0.25/ride with ID; Calvin $0.75/ride with Calvin Wave card.

## Key Rider Info

- **Rapid Central Station**, 250 Cesar E Chavez Ave SW, is the downtown hub — most routes
  start/end there. Information Center inside (Mon-Fri 7:00-17:00, 616-776-1100).
- **No service holidays:** New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day,
  Thanksgiving, Christmas.
- **Real-time bus tracking:** Transit app, myStop app, or the system map at ridetherapid.org.
- All buses have front bike racks and are wheelchair accessible.
- Board at the front door (rear-door boarding allowed on Laker Line only).
- **GO!Bus** (ADA paratransit, 616-456-6141) and **PASS** (suburban feeder shuttle, $3.50,
  616-459-7701) require advance registration.
- There is **no summer service** on Route 60 (GRCC) or Route 100 (FSU Bulldog Line).
